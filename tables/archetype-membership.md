# Archetype Membership

This document lists the 317 archetype studies of the survey grouped by archetype and sub-group, followed by the 33 benchmark and evaluation studies. Within each group, works are ordered alphabetically by first author. Each reference identifies the published version by its DOI; where no DOI is available, as for most machine-learning conference proceedings, the arXiv identifier of the corresponding preprint is given where one exists. The classification of each work is recorded in [`../data/corpus.csv`](../data/corpus.csv) and defined in [`../method/coding-scheme.md`](../method/coding-scheme.md).
